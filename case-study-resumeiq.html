ResumeIQ — Career Intelligence Platform

An evidence-based career intelligence platform engineered to demystify the automated resume screening process by mapping exact bullet-point evidence against job description requirements.

Role Lead Product Designer & Frontend Developer
Scope UX Research, Information Architecture, Component System, Interactive Prototyping
Tools & Stack Figma, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PDF.js
Platform Responsive Web Application (Desktop & Mobile)
01 / Overview

Product Vision & Executive Summary

Early-career candidates face a frustrating "black box" when applying to modern tech roles. Traditional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and algorithmic screeners filter out qualified applicants who fail to use exact terminology or structure their achievements with quantifiable impact.

ResumeIQ was built to replace stressful, arbitrary scores with a calm, transparent dashboard that answers five critical questions for the applicant:

1. Alignment

How clearly does my resume communicate relevance to this specific role?

2. Evidence

Which required competencies are substantiated by quantifiable accomplishments?

3. Skill Gaps

Which critical skills are missing or weakly articulated in my current draft?

4. Actionable Diff

What concrete phrasing transformations will make my achievements clearer?

02 / Problem Discovery

The Problem: Why Existing Resume Tools Fail Job Seekers

Through user research interviews with university students and career switchers, three critical failures of existing commercial resume checkers were uncovered:

Pain Point 1

Judgmental, Anxiety-Inducing Scoring

Tools display an arbitrary red "62/100" without explaining what the number means or how it was calculated, inducing imposter syndrome rather than empowerment.

Pain Point 2

Dangerous Fabrication Incentives

Generic tools prompt candidates to blindly copy-paste missing keywords into their resume, encouraging exaggeration that leads to failed technical interviews.

Pain Point 3

Vague, Non-Contextual Advice

Advice like "Add more action verbs" does not teach candidates how to rephrase their specific coursework or project bullets to demonstrate impact.

04 / Design Principles

Core Principles Guiding Every Screen

Principle UX Execution in ResumeIQ
Reassuring Over Judgmental Scores are framed as "Alignment with Target Job Description" rather than an assessment of candidate worth. Disclaimers explicitly state that algorithms do not decide human potential.
Evidence-Backed Citations Every matched competency highlights the exact line from the job posting and the exact bullet from the uploaded resume. Zero black-box claims.
Actionable Before/After Diffs Recommendations present real phrasing transformations following the formula: [Action Verb] + [What Was Built] + [Quantified Outcome].
Zero-Fabrication Guardrail Proactive alerts remind applicants never to claim unearned skills, focusing instead on clearly articulating genuine coursework and completed projects.
Privacy by Architecture 100% client-side parsing via browser PDF.js and local storage. No resumes or personal contact details are transmitted to external servers.
05 / Information Architecture

End-to-End User Journey

The candidate journey was designed to minimize friction and provide immediate clarity in under 30 seconds:

Step 1 Document Intake

Drag-and-drop resume upload or selection of pre-configured sample profiles for instant exploration.

Step 2 Target Role Input

Paste target job description or select curated tech roles (Frontend, UI/UX, Data).

Step 3 Evidence Dashboard

Explore overall match readiness, keyword alignment, and verified evidence badges.

Step 4 Skill Gap Matrix

Inspect missing must-haves vs bonus qualifications with inline learning resources.

Step 5 Recommendation Diff

Apply suggested bullet rewrites and export an ATS-optimized revision.

06 / Interface Design

Key Interaction Decisions & UI Polish

The interface was built around dark-surface ergonomics (`#0A0F1E` navy canvas with `#6366F1` electric indigo accents) to reduce visual fatigue during intensive resume editing.

1. Micro-Feedback on Document Upload

Rather than a static spinner, an interactive step indicator shows real-time extraction progress: Reading PDF → Detecting Sections → Mapping Keywords → Calculating Alignment. This creates confidence in algorithmic fidelity.

2. Triple Score Segmentation

The overall score is decoupled into three transparent sub-scores: Core Competencies (92%), Quantified Impact (84%), and ATS Formatting (90%), allowing candidates to tackle improvements systematically.

3. Inline Phrasing Diffs

Instead of abstract advice, the rewriter displays a side-by-side color-coded diff showing exactly how passive verbs are replaced with high-impact engineering accomplishments.

4. Multi-Job Benchmarking

Candidates can test their current resume against multiple postings concurrently to identify whether their profile is stronger for frontend engineering or product design.

07 / Accessibility & Code Quality

Accessibility Considerations & Technical Execution

As a UI/UX designer with frontend development expertise, accessible execution was a primary requirement:

  • WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast Compliance: All text surfaces maintain a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against dark navy surfaces.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Full tab-index traversal with visible electric-indigo focus rings for power users.
  • Semantic HTML & ARIA: Progress indicators, tabs, and modal dialogues feature appropriate ARIA live regions and dialog semantics.
  • Responsive Ergonomics: The layout fluidly adapts from multi-column desktop monitoring to a focused, vertical mobile flow.

Design Hypotheses & Next Validation Steps
