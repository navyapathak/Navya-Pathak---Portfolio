UI/UX Case Study · Personal Concept Project

ResumeIQ — Career Intelligence Dashboard

A comprehensive human-centered design concept created to transform how job seekers navigate ATS filtering, understand skill gaps, and improve resume relevance with explainable AI feedback.

My Role Product Designer & Frontend Architect
Design Tools Figma, Space Grotesk, Inter, CSS
Implementation Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind
Scope End-to-End UX & Prototyping

01 The Problem Statement

Over 75% of qualified job seekers receive automated rejections from Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) without ever understanding why. Current resume review tools either offer superficial "keyword density" checkers or opaque AI scores that induce anxiety rather than offering actionable direction.

Core Design Challenge: How might we design an interface that gives candidates complete transparency into their match score, demystifies ATS algorithms, and provides clear, non-overwhelming steps to improve?

02 Intended Users & Personas

I identified two primary user segments who suffer most from ATS opacity:

Persona A: Ananya (Recent CS Graduate)

Goal: Secure an internship or entry-level role.
Pain Point: Unsure whether her academic projects align with industry job postings; sends 50+ applications without feedback.

Persona B: Rohan (Front-End Transitioner)

Goal: Transition from pure design to UI/UX Engineer.
Pain Point: Possesses transferable coding skills but struggles to structure resume bullet points with quantified engineering metrics.

03 Core Design Goals

1. Transparency

Never show an opaque number. Every score must explain its underlying factors (skills, experience, formatting).

2. Actionability

Transform every identified gap into an immediate action: e.g., an inline bullet-point rewrite suggestion.

3. Calm Ergonomics

Job hunting is high-stress. The UI uses deep navy surfaces, electric indigo accents, and generous whitespace to reduce cognitive strain.

04 User Flow & Information Architecture

The candidate journey is streamlined into five clear stages from upload to revised application:

STEP 01
Resume Upload & Role Selection
STEP 02
Semantic Parsing & Feature Extraction
STEP 03
Dashboard Overview & Match Score
STEP 04
Skill-Gap Analysis & Rewriter
STEP 05
1-Click Export & Re-Evaluation

Designing ResumeIQ reinforced that AI in product design must always be explainable. When users understand why a score is assigned, anxiety gives way to empowerment.
