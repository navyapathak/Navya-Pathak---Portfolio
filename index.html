Methodology

My design process.

A structured, iterative approach that connects human empathy and problem framing with code-ready frontend execution.

01 Empathy

Discover

Uncovering real user friction before jumping to solutions. Listening to user frustrations, conducting contextual inquiries, and studying workflow bottlenecks.

  • User Interviews & Contextual Inquiry
  • Workflow Friction Mapping
  • Competitive Heuristic Analysis
02 Strategy

Define

Transforming raw findings into clear problem statements, actionable user personas, and defensible design hypotheses with measurable success criteria.

  • Problem Statement Framing
  • User Personas & Mental Models
  • Information Architecture (IA)
03 Ideation

Explore

Rapidly iterating through low-fidelity wireframes, user flow diagrams, and multiple layout variations to discover the most intuitive structural pathways.

  • Low-Fidelity Wireframing
  • User Flow Diagrams
  • Layout & Hierarchy Explorations
04 Execution

Prototype

Bridging design and engineering. Crafting high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma and translating them into responsive, production-ready React/TypeScript interfaces.

  • High-Fidelity Component Systems
  • Figma Auto-Layout & Variants
  • Functional Code Prototyping (React)
05 Testing

Validate

Subjecting designs to cognitive walkthroughs, task completion evaluation, and strict accessibility auditing (WCAG 2.1 AA) before shipping.

  • Usability Task Benchmarking
  • Accessibility & Contrast Audits
  • Heuristic Evaluation
06 Polish

Refine

Polishing microinteractions, edge cases, loading skeletons, and responsive breakpoints to deliver a cohesive, delight-inducing product experience.

  • Microinteractions & Transitions
  • Error & Empty State Design
  • Design Tokens & Tokenization

UI/UX Case Study · Quick In-Page Preview

ResumeIQ — Career Intelligence Dashboard

A comprehensive human-centered design concept created to transform how job seekers navigate ATS filtering, understand skill gaps, and improve resume relevance with explainable AI feedback.

My Role Product Designer & Frontend Developer
Design Tools Figma, Space Grotesk, Inter, CSS
Implementation React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
Full Document Open Dedicated Page →

01 The Problem Statement

Over 75% of qualified job seekers receive automated rejections from Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) without ever understanding why. Current resume review tools either offer superficial "keyword density" checkers or opaque AI scores that induce anxiety rather than offering actionable direction.

Core Design Challenge: How might we design an interface that gives candidates complete transparency into their match score, demystifies ATS algorithms, and provides clear, non-overwhelming steps to improve?

02 Intended Users & Personas

Persona A: Ananya (Recent CS Graduate)

Goal: Secure an internship or entry-level role.
Pain Point: Unsure whether her academic projects align with industry job postings; sends 50+ applications without feedback.

Persona B: Rohan (Front-End Transitioner)

Goal: Transition from pure design to UI/UX Engineer.
Pain Point: Possesses transferable coding skills but struggles to structure resume bullet points with quantified engineering metrics.
