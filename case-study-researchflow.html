ResearchFlow — AI Research Workspace

An AI research workspace designed to make document exploration, academic literature synthesis, and source verification clearer, traceable, and intuitive through synchronized split-screen interaction.

Role Lead Product Designer & UX Architect
Scope AI Interaction Design, Citation Architecture, Dual-Pane Workspace Layout
Tools & Stack Figma, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Local IndexedDB
Project Type Verified High-Fidelity Prototype & System Design
01 / Overview

Product Vision: Solving the "Epistemic Trust" Crisis in AI

Generative AI chatbots have unlocked immense speed for text summarization, but have introduced a critical UX breakdown: answers are completely decoupled from their source documents. Researchers receive plausible-sounding answers with no clear way to verify whether the AI hallucinated the claims or cited legitimate empirical data.

ResearchFlow was designed around a fundamental UX principle: Place the document and its verifiable evidence at the absolute center of the experience.

1. Synchronized View

Split-screen workspace linking conversation prompts directly to document pages.

2. Bidirectional Citations

Clicking any citation badge auto-jumps the reader and pulses the exact evidentiary paragraph.

3. Anti-Hallucination Disclosures

Explicit warnings when uploaded documents lack sufficient empirical evidence to answer.

4. Zero-Lock-In Privacy

Local client-side caching via IndexedDB ensuring sensitive research papers never leak.

02 / Problem Discovery

The Friction: Why Academic Researchers Distrust Generic AI

Interviews with student researchers, thesis candidates, and literature review authors revealed three persistent pain points in traditional conversational AI tools:

Pain Point 1

Context Switching Tax

Users constantly toggle between a PDF reader tab and a chat interface, manually searching for keywords to verify claims made by the model.

Pain Point 2

Missing Methodological Nuance

Generic summaries blur critical experimental conditions, hyperparameters, benchmark datasets, and sample sizes necessary for rigorous research.

Pain Point 3

Opaque Ingestion States

Users are left guessing whether long PDF appendices, tables, and mathematical formulas were indexed or silently dropped during upload.

03 / Target Audience

Primary User Segments

🔬

The Graduate Researcher

Goal: Conducting systematic literature reviews across 30+ peer-reviewed papers.

Need: Must cross-reference contradictory findings between papers and trace specific mathematical equations back to the original authors.

📚

The Engineering Student

Goal: Reviewing foundational computer science papers (e.g., Attention Is All You Need).

Need: Wants an intuitive reading companion that clarifies complex terminology without oversimplifying or fabricating findings.

04 / Design Principles

Core UX Principles for AI Interaction

Principle UX Execution in ResearchFlow
Grounding Before Synthesis Synthesized responses always lead with citations and confidence metrics before prose explanations.
Bidirectional Traceability Every citation is clickable; the PDF viewport immediately scrolls to the exact paragraph and applies a subtle glowing pulse.
Transparent Ingestion Pipeline Upload modal breaks parsing into visible stages: Parsing PDF → Chunking Sections → Vector Indexing → Ready.
Honest Negative Proofs When a query cannot be answered by the uploaded documents, ResearchFlow issues an amber notice rather than hallucinating an answer.
Cognitive Calmness Deep navy surfaces (`#0A0F1E`) paired with electric indigo highlights create a distraction-free environment for intensive reading sessions.
05 / Information Architecture

Split-Screen Workspace Architecture

The layout solves the context-switching penalty by placing the reading canvas and conversational intelligence side-by-side:

Left Sidebar Document Library

Active document scopes, multi-doc selectors, session history, and IndexedDB cache indicators.

Center Pane Synchronized Reader

Page navigation, in-document search, zoom controls, and interactive highlight pulses triggered by chat.

Right Pane Research Conversation

Citation cards with match percentages, executive syntheses, and suggested follow-up queries.

06 / Key Interactions

Key Interaction Decisions & Edge Case Handling

1. Citation Auto-Scroll with Pulsing Glow

Clicking [1] Attention Is All You Need • Page 1 in the chat triggers smooth auto-scrolling on the left reader canvas and flashes a soft indigo pulse around the matching paragraph for 1.8 seconds.

2. Transparent "Non-Speculation" Alert

If a question asks about details not present in the ingested papers (e.g. training compute costs), the UI surfaces an honest badge: "No direct evidence found in active documents. Model refused speculation to prevent hallucination."

3. Curated Starter Papers for Instant Testing

To eliminate empty-state friction, ResearchFlow comes pre-loaded with canonical open-access papers (Vaswani et al. Transformer paper, Climate Action reports) allowing recruiters to immediately experience the synchronized workflow.

4. Follow-Up Query Suggestions

Each synthesis concludes with three contextual inquiry chips (e.g., "How does Scaled Dot-Product Attention prevent vanishing gradients?"), guiding the user into deeper literature exploration.

07 / Accessibility & Code Quality

Accessibility & Frontend Engineering Standards

  • Keyboard Focus Traversal: Researchers can navigate from query input to citation badges and reader pages entirely using standard keyboard shortcuts.
  • High Contrast Ratio: Typography in Inter and Space Grotesk adheres to strict WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines on dark surface tokens.
  • Responsive Collapsible Panes: On tablet viewports, the side panels collapse into accessible drawer tabs to maximize reading real estate.
  • Client-Side Storage: Utilizes browser IndexedDB for instant session reloads without requiring server round-trips.
08 / Reflection & Validation

Prototype Status & Future Iterations

Honest Prototype Labeling

ResearchFlow is an interactive product design prototype demonstrating innovative UX patterns for citation-grounded research. It uses client-side simulated retrieval to illustrate split-screen synchronization, citation jumping, and anti-hallucination states.

Next Iterations:
  • Integration with local WebAssembly-based vector embeddings for offline PDF search.
  • Multi-document comparative matrix allowing simultaneous side-by-side citation across two research papers.
  • Exporting verified literature review sections with BibTeX citations.
